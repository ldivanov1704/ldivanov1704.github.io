Privacy policy

Mail agent is used only by its owner, on his own Google account.

  • It reads the owner's mail to label, archive and summarise it for him.
  • Data is not sold, shared or used for advertising, and no other person's account is accessed.
  • Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
